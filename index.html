Season 1
Season Overview & Timeline
Season 1: "Winter Is Coming" is built around temperature, Season Resistance, and city control.
Core Mechanics
  • Cold Snap (Week 1–7): Periodic temperature drops divided into 12 levels.
  • City Unlocks: Lv.1–2 Week 1, Lv.3–4 Week 2, Lv.5–6 Week 3, Lv.7 Week 4.
  • City Race: Unions compete for Ice Crystals.
  • Kingdom Expedition: Cross-kingdom occupation with 4 city cap.
  • Royal City Clash: Main battlefield event.
  • Hero Systems: Promotion, Level Swap, Season Boost.
Pro Tip: Cancel march to unfrozen target to keep squad with speed boost
Thermal Furnace & Season Resistance
Your core building for surviving Season 1. Controls castle temperature and unlocks key milestones.
Week 1 Priorities
  • Upgrade Thermal Furnace as top priority
  • Upgrade Mithril Workshop
  • Activate Season Weekly Card (+250 Season Resistance)
  • Upgrade Union Furnace for land temperature
Milestones
  • Lv.12+: Basic stability by Week 1
  • Lv.20: Unlocks Halls of War
  • Lv.30: Season progression & Level Swap rewards
⚠️ If Furnace falls behind Cold Snaps, nothing else matters
Union Furnace & Land Temperature
Raises land temperature around union territory. Critical for preventing freezing during Cold Snaps.
Function
  • Increases land temperature in radius
  • Requires daily Coal & Gold donations
  • Relocate strategically before Cold Snaps
🔥 Position it where the fighting is, not where it started
Fuel, Coal & Snowland Treasure
Freezing Rules
  • Castle freezes at -20°C or below
  • Can still: Kill Frost Zombies, farm, plunder, complete some quests
  • Cannot: Fight Elite Zombies, launch rallies, join Union Boss
Snowland Treasure
  • Appears at 0°C or higher
  • Up to 5 rewards per day
  • Raise temp: capture city, move Furnace, use Meteor Strike
Farm Escape
  • New stage unlocks daily
  • Primary Coal source for Weeks 1–3
💰 Skip these and stay poor in the one resource that keeps you alive
Cold Snap Levels & Weekly Drops
Weekly Intensity
  • Week 1: Early snaps, activate Overload
  • Week 2: Up to -30°C
  • Week 3: Up to -50°C
  • Week 5: Up to -90°C
  • Week 6: Up to -110°C
  • Week 7: Ultimate snap, up to -130°C
Prep Before Snaps
  • Raise castle temp in advance
  • Relocate to higher-temp zones
  • Ensure both Furnaces active
  • R5s move rally point to safe zones
❄️ Cold Snaps are announced — if you freeze, it's neglect not surprise
Temperature & Season Resistance
Stats Impact
  • Directly affects battle performance
  • Upgrade Thermal Furnace & Mithril Workshop
  • Season Weekly Card: +250 Resistance
Season Boost Items
  • Skin: Snowy Night Parade
  • Heroes: Crystal, Sera, Fenixia, Daphne
  • Decoration: Order Spire
⚡ Resistance isn't luxury — it's the difference between full power and numb hands
Cities, Mines & City Race
City Race
  • Union Rank: Total Ice Crystals from all members
  • Kingdom Rank: Sum of all unions' Ice Crystals
  • Rewards at settlement based on rankings
Unlock Schedule
  • Week 1: Lv.1–2 cities
  • Week 2: Lv.3–4 cities
  • Week 3: Lv.5–6 cities
  • Week 4: Lv.7 Royal City
Mine Limits
  • Base: 3 mines
  • Lv.1–5 city: +1 per city
  • Lv.6 city: +2 per city
  • Royal City: No bonus
👑 City Race decides your mine cap
Royal City Clash
Same as previous season with one change: four Lv.6 cities instead of three.
Rules
  • Royal City gives NO Ice Crystal points
  • Not contestable by other servers
  • Must hold ≥1 Lv.6 city or become Kingless
⚔️ Lose all Lv.6 cities = lose your King
Kingdom Expedition
Cross-kingdom city occupation from a fixed Outpost.
Mechanics (Week 3–7)
  • Set Outpost in target kingdom (permanent)
  • Max 4 cities in Expedition
  • Total cap: 8 cities (4 own + 4 Expedition)
⚠️ Bad Outpost placement = bad season decisions locked in
Kingdom War Declaration
Changes how often you can declare war on cities.
Declaration Rules (Week 4–7)
  • Before Event: 3 declarations per day
  • During Event: 2 chances per Wednesday & Saturday only
  • Total daily: 2 declarations (all kingdoms combined)
⏰ Your real resource: declaration slots, not troops
Clash of Kingdoms
Same as Season 0 with Royal City as main battlefield.
Behavior
  • Slower attacks, rallies, scouting
  • Slower healing allies' castles
  • ⚠️ Don't teleport with Expedition during Clash hour
  • Use traditional Attack/Defend buttons
🚫 Kingdom Expedition teleport = opting out of Royal City fight
Hero Systems & Boosts
Hero Promotion (Week 2–7)
  • 5-star Crystal → S+ tier hero
  • Keep heroes idle before promoting
  • Honor Wall shards refunded if unlocked
  • S+ upgrades cost 2x S-tier shards
  • Remains S+ after season ends
Level Swap (Week 1–7)
  • Transfer levels between any heroes
  • Star tier & gear unaffected
  • Get vouchers: Furnace Lv.5 & Lv.30 rewards
  • Best: high-level low-priority → low-level core DPS
🔧 These fix bad early investments
Final Week & Settlement
Week 7: Final Push
  • Final Kingdom Expedition war day
  • Max Halls of War buildings
  • Final City Race push
  • Clash of Kingdoms settlements
  • Union Duel League final push
  • Ultimate Cold Snap: -130°C
Week 8: Settlement
  • Rewards based on Union & Kingdom ranks
  • R5s distribute to members
  • All get kingdom rank via mail
  • Snowland Treasure still available
🎁 Cash out on your smart decisions from Week 1
