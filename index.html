Season 1: "Winter Is Coming"

8.1 — Season Overview & Timeline

Season 1: "Winter Is Coming" is built around temperature, Season Resistance, and city control. Cold Snaps, city unlock phases, and cross‑kingdom events all stack together, so your survival depends on planning week by week instead of treating it like a normal season.

Core seasonal mechanics

  • Cold Snap (Week 1–7): Periodic temperature drops divided into 12 levels, with varying durations and countdowns before and during each snap.
  • City Unlocks: Lv.1–2 cities in Week 1, Lv.3–4 in Week 2, Lv.5–6 in Week 3, and the Lv.7 Royal City in Week 4.
  • City Race: Unions and kingdoms compete for Ice Crystals based on city and mine occupation across the season.
  • Kingdom Expedition: Cross‑kingdom city and mine occupation with a hard cap of 4 cities per union in Expedition.
  • Royal City Clash & Clash of Kingdoms: Royal City battlefield plus bracketed kingdom‑vs‑kingdom war.
  • Hero Systems: Hero Promotion (Crystal to S+), Level Swap, and Season Boost decorations/heroes.

"S1 Pro Tip: cancelling your march to an unfrozen target returns your squad while maintaining the accelerated speed boost."

8.2 — Thermal Furnace & Season Resistance

Your Thermal Furnace is the core building for surviving Season 1. It controls your castle temperature, unlocks key milestones, and ties directly into Season Resistance and progression.

Early‑season priorities (Week 1)

  • Upgrade your Thermal Furnace as a top priority — it directly affects your survival and combat performance.
  • Upgrade your Mithril Workshop and consider activating the Season Weekly Card for an extra workshop and +250 Season Resistance.
  • Upgrade your Union Furnace to raise land temperature around your hive and donate Coal and Gold daily.

Progression milestones

  • At Thermal Furnace Lv.12+: recommended by Week 1 for basic stability.
  • At Thermal Furnace Lv.20: unlocks the Halls of War.
  • At Thermal Furnace Lv.30: key milestone for Season progression and Level Swap rewards.

"If your Thermal Furnace isn’t keeping up with the week’s Cold Snap, nothing else you build will matter."

8.3 — Union Furnace & Land Temperature

The Union Furnace raises land temperature around your union’s territory and is critical for keeping members from freezing, especially during deeper Cold Snaps and city pushes.

Function & usage

  • Increases land temperature in its radius, helping castles resist freezing and enabling events like Snowland Treasure.
  • Requires daily donations of Coal and Gold to level up.
  • Should be relocated strategically before major Cold Snaps and city pushes.

Weekly focus

  • Week 1–2: Steadily upgrade Union Furnace and keep it active as Cold Snaps intensify.
  • Before big Cold Snaps: R5s should move the union rally point and Furnace to high‑temperature zones.
  • During city pushes: Position it to cover as many active fighters as possible.

"A high‑level Union Furnace is wasted if it’s sitting in the wrong place — move the heat where the fighting is."

8.4 — Fuel, Coal Sources & Snowland Treasure

Cold Snap & freezing rules

  • Cold Snaps significantly lower kingdom temperature and are divided into 12 levels with varying durations.
  • Your castle freezes at -20°C or below. While frozen, you can still:
    • Kill Frost Zombies
    • Farm resources and kill normal zombies
    • Plunder Wagons
    • Complete some intelligence quests (except rally‑required zombies)
    • Do Acorn Tavern plunder quests
    • Participate in City War declarations (R4–R5 only)
  • While frozen, you cannot:
    • Fight Elite Zombies
    • Launch rallies
    • Join Union Boss events

Snowland Treasure (Week 1–8)

  • Mysterious treasures appear when land temperature reaches 0°C or higher.
  • Break the ice and claim up to 5 treasure rewards per day.
  • Capturing a city reveals how many treasures remain in its land.
  • Raise land temperature by:
    • Capturing the city
    • Moving and activating your Union Furnace there
    • Using the class skill Meteor Strike (when available)

Farm Escape (Week 1–8)

  • Mini‑game where a new stage unlocks daily.
  • Clearing stages grants resources, including Coal.
  • One of the primary Coal sources in early Season 1, especially Weeks 1–3.

"If you’re ignoring Farm Escape and Snowland Treasure, you’re choosing to be poor in the one resource that keeps you alive."

8.5 — Cold Snap Levels & Weekly Drops

Cold Snaps escalate over the season, with each week dropping kingdom temperature more aggressively. You’re given countdowns before and during each snap, but if you don’t act on them, you freeze.

Weekly Cold Snap intensity

  • Week 1: Early Cold Snaps; recommended to activate Overlode mode and keep both Thermal and Union Furnaces running.
  • Week 2: Cold Snap can drop temperatures by up to 30°C.
  • Week 3: Cold Snap can drop temperatures by up to 50°C.
  • Week 5: Cold Snap can drop temperatures by up to 90°C.
  • Week 6: Cold Snap can drop temperatures by up to 110°C.
  • Week 7 (Ultimate Cold Snap): Temperatures can drop by up to 130°C.

Preparation checklist before each Cold Snap

  • Raise your castle temperature in advance — don’t wait for the drop.
  • Relocate to higher‑temperature zones if needed.
  • Ensure Thermal Furnace and Union Furnace are activated.
  • R5s should move the union rally point to safer zones before major drops.

"Cold Snaps are announced in advance — if you freeze, it’s not surprise, it’s neglect."

8.6 — Temperature, Season Resistance & Seasonal Boosts

Temperature & Season Resistance

  • In Season 1, temperature and Season Resistance directly affect your battle performance.
  • Upgrading the Thermal Furnace and Mithril Workshop is essential to maintain combat strength.
  • The Season Weekly Card grants an extra Mithril Workshop and +250 Season Resistance.

Season Boost (Week 1–7)

  • Extra buffs are granted when using specific decorations and heroes:
    • Castle Skin: Snowy Night Parade
    • Heroes: Crystal, Sera, Fenixia, Daphne
    • Decoration: Order Spire

"Season Resistance isn’t a luxury stat in Season 1 — it’s the difference between fighting at full power and swinging with numb hands."

8.7 — Cities, Mines & City Race

City Race (Week 1–7)

  • Union ranking: Unions compete based on total Ice Crystals obtained throughout the season.
  • Kingdom ranking: A kingdom’s total Ice Crystals equal the sum of all unions’ Ice Crystals in that kingdom.
  • Rewards are distributed at settlement based on these rankings.

City unlock schedule

  • Week 1: Lv.1–2 cities
  • Week 2: Lv.3–4 cities
  • Week 3: Lv.5–6 cities
  • Week 4: Lv.7 Royal City

Mine limits from city occupation

  • Base mine limit: 3.
  • Lv.1–5 cities: +1 mine limit per city.
  • Lv.6 cities: +2 mine limit per city.
  • Royal City: no mine limit bonus.
  • If you lose a city, your mine limit drops, but you keep existing mines unless you manually abandon them.

"City Race isn’t just about bragging rights — it quietly decides how many mines your union is allowed to own."

8.8 — Royal City Clash

Royal City Clash in Season 1 works the same as the previous season, with one key change: there are now four Lv.6 cities instead of three.

Royal City rules

  • Royal City does not provide Ice Crystal points or occupation rewards.
  • Royal City is not contestable by other servers.
  • Your server must occupy at least one Lv.6 city in your own kingdom during Kingdom Expedition, or:
    • Your server cannot occupy its own Royal City.
    • Your server will be Kingless and unable to use titles.

"If your server fails to hold a single Lv.6 city, you don’t just lose a fight — you lose your King."

8.9 — Kingdom Expedition

Kingdom Expedition lets unions project power into other kingdoms, occupying their cities and mines from a fixed Outpost.

Core mechanics (Week 3–7)

  • Unions set an Outpost in another kingdom as a permanent starting point.
  • The Outpost is immovable once placed, so choose the target kingdom and location carefully.
  • Your union can occupy a maximum of 4 cities in Kingdom Expedition.
  • Total city cap: 8 cities (4 in your own kingdom + 4 in Expedition).

"A badly placed Outpost locks your entire union into a bad decision for the rest of the event."

8.10 — Kingdom War Declaration

Kingdom War Declaration changes how often you can declare war on cities and when those declarations happen.

Declaration rules (Week 4–7)

  • Before Kingdom War Declaration begins:
    • Your union has 3 war declarations per day for cities.
  • After the event begins:
    • City War Declarations are limited to 2 chances per Wednesday and Saturday only.
    • The total daily number of declarations (your own kingdom + other kingdoms) is 2 on those days.

"In Kingdom War Declaration, your real resource isn’t troops — it’s declaration slots."

8.11 — Clash of Kingdoms

Clash of Kingdoms in Season 1 works the same as Season 0, with the Royal City as the main battlefield even before your server holds a Lv.6 city.

Behavior & restrictions

  • Expect slower movements for attacks, rallies, and scouting to castles, guardians, and the Royal City.
  • Heating allies’ castles is also slower during Clash of Kingdoms.
  • Important: Do not use Kingdom Expedition teleportation during Clash of Kingdoms hour, or you won’t be able to participate in attacking/reinforcing the Royal City.
  • Use the traditional Attack or Defend buttons to avoid bugs.

"If you teleport with Kingdom Expedition during Clash of Kingdoms, you’re opting out of the main fight by accident."

8.12 — Hero Systems: Promotion, Level Swap & Seasonal Buffs

Hero Promotion (Week 2–7)

  • During the event, 5‑star Crystal can be promoted to an S+ tier hero.
  • Make sure heroes are idle (no Tavern quests, Wagon quests, or rallies) before promoting.
  • If her Honor Wall was unlocked before promotion, those shards are refunded via in‑game mail.
  • Upgrading S+ Crystal still uses S‑tier shards, but at double cost.
  • If you promote Crystal during the event, she remains S+ after the season ends.

Level Swap (Week 1–7)

  • Consume a Level Swap Voucher to transfer levels between any two heroes.
  • Star tier and gear level are unaffected, but some previously unlocked skills may relock.
  • Vouchers are obtained from:
    • Goals → Build Furnace → Lv.5 Rewards
    • Goals → Build Furnace → Lv.30 Rewards
  • Best used by swapping levels from a high‑level, low‑priority hero to a low‑level core DPS or tank.

Season Boost recap

  • Extra buffs from:
    • Castle Skin: Snowy Night Parade
    • Heroes: Crystal, Sera, Fenixia, Daphne
    • Decoration: Order Spire

"Level Swap and Hero Promotion are how you fix bad early investments — if you ignore them, you’re stuck with your mistakes all season."

8.13 — Final Week, Settlement & Checklist

Week 7: Final push

  • Final war day for Season 1 Kingdom Expedition — Halls of War buildings should be high level, especially your main faction.
  • Final push for City Race — optimize city and mine captures for leaderboard settlement.
  • Clash of Kingdoms Conqueror — final settlement for bracket winners.
  • Union Duel League — final push for VS event points and rewards.
  • Ultimate Cold Snap — up to 130°C temperature drop; raise castle temperature in time.

Week 8: Season settlement

  • Season settlement distributes rewards based on Union and Kingdom rankings.
  • R5s receive union rank rewards and manually distribute member rewards.
  • All lords receive their kingdom rank rewards via in‑game mail.
  • You can still experience Snowland Treasure during this period.

"By Week 8, you’re not grinding — you’re cashing out on every smart decision you made from Week 1 onward."
